How Junk Removal Is Priced in Miami

Most junk removal companies in Miami price by volume — meaning how much space your items take up in the truck. Miami Junk Removal Pros uses the same approach. The more truck space your junk fills, the higher the price. This is fairer than hourly pricing because you pay for what you have, not how long it takes us to work.

Several factors affect your final price:

  • Volume — how much space your items take up in our truck (the biggest factor)
  • Weight — heavy items like concrete, tile, and appliances cost more to dispose of
  • Access — items on upper floors, in tight spaces, or requiring disassembly take more labor
  • Type of material — hazardous materials, electronics, and appliances with refrigerants have special disposal fees
  • Location — we serve all of Miami-Dade and Broward; travel surcharges may apply for distant locations like Boca Raton

The best way to get an accurate price is to text us photos at (305) 928-5773. We look at the photos, estimate the volume and labor, and send you a firm price — usually within 10 minutes.

What Is NOT Included in Standard Pricing

We are transparent about what costs extra so there are no surprises:

  • Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, asbestos, biohazards require specialized disposal and are not included in standard pricing
  • High-rise surcharge — buildings above 10 floors without service elevator access may have an additional labor fee
  • Travel surcharge — Palm Beach County locations (Boca Raton, Delray Beach) may have a travel fee disclosed upfront in your quote
  • After-hours service — jobs outside 7AM–7PM standard hours may have an additional fee
  • Extremely heavy items — concrete, large safes, and piano grands require special equipment and are priced separately

Frequently Asked Questions — Junk Removal Pricing Miami

How is junk removal pricing calculated?

Pricing is based mainly on volume — how much space your items take up in the truck. Weight, access, and disposal needs can also affect the total. Single items typically cost $125 – $175, small loads $200 – $300, medium loads $300 – $450, and full truck loads $450 – $650. We confirm the final price on-site before work begins.

Do you charge extra for heavy items?

Most appliances and large furniture are included in standard single-item or load pricing. Extremely heavy items like concrete, large safes, or grand pianos may have an additional fee. If that applies, we tell you before we start — no surprises.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes. Our minimum charge is $125. That covers a single small item or a very small amount of miscellaneous junk.

Do you charge for stairs or difficult access?

Most walk-up, elevator, and standard access jobs are included in your quote. High-rise buildings without a service elevator or unusually difficult access may include an additional labor fee, which we disclose before work begins.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, Zelle, Venmo, and all major credit cards. Payment is due when the job is completed.
