Why Miami Junk Removal Pros Exists

Wilder Etienne grew up in Allapattah and has spent the last 20 years living back and forth between Allapattah and North Miami. These are his neighborhoods. He knows the streets, the people, and the problems.

In 2025, Wilder kept seeing the same thing — bulk trash piling up across North Miami. Furniture on sidewalks. Appliances sitting in alleys. Properties that needed clearing but had no affordable, reliable option. The big national junk removal franchises weren't built for communities like his. They were expensive, slow to respond, and disconnected from the people they were supposed to serve.

So Wilder built Miami Junk Removal Pros — a fast, honest, affordable junk removal service designed specifically for Miami-Dade and Broward residents. No call centers. No franchise fees passed on to customers. Just a local team that shows up, does the work, and treats every client and every property with respect.

Who We Are

Miami Junk Removal Pros is a Black-owned, Haitian-American business founded and operated by Wilder Etienne. Wilder runs day-to-day operations with the help of two cousins who have been part of the team since day one — a tight family unit built on reliability, hard work, and community pride.

We are not a franchise. We are not a call center that dispatches strangers. When you call or text Miami Junk Removal Pros, you are reaching Wilder directly. When our crew shows up at your property, it is people who care about doing the job right — because this business is our reputation and our livelihood.

Being Haitian-American and rooted in Miami for over 20 years means we understand this city in ways that national companies never will. We know North Miami, Little Haiti, Allapattah, Hialeah, and every neighborhood in between. We speak the language of this community — literally and figuratively.

We provide same-day junk removal and Airbnb turnover cleaning across Miami-Dade and Broward County. What makes us different from every other junk removal company in Miami is simple: we are the only service that combines junk removal AND Airbnb turnover cleaning in one visit. Airbnb hosts and property managers across Miami rely on us because we eliminate the need to coordinate two separate vendors. One call. One team. One invoice.

We operate 7AM to 7PM, seven days a week. We respond to texts in under 10 minutes. We give firm quotes before we start — no surprises, no hidden fees. And we treat every home, every estate, and every rental property with the care and respect it deserves.

Our Commitment to Miami-Dade and Broward

Miami Junk Removal Pros was built in this community and exists to serve it. We donate usable furniture and household items to Miami-area charities whenever possible. We dispose of appliances and electronics responsibly, working with certified recycling facilities in South Florida. We keep as much out of the landfill as we can — because these are our neighborhoods too and we want them clean and healthy.

When you hire Miami Junk Removal Pros, you are not sending money to a national franchise headquartered in another state. You are supporting a local family business that is invested in the same community you live in. That matters to us — and we think it should matter to you too.
