What Is an Estate Cleanout?

An estate cleanout is the process of clearing out an entire property — typically after the passing of a loved one, a major life transition, or in preparation for selling a home. It's one of the most emotionally demanding tasks a family can face, and it often comes with a tight deadline imposed by probate courts, real estate closings, or rental agreements.

Miami Junk Removal Pros handles estate cleanouts with the care and discretion the situation deserves. We move efficiently without being rushed, we treat every item with respect, and we keep you informed throughout the process. Our goal is to take this burden off your plate completely so you can focus on what matters.

Who We Work With

We regularly partner with and serve:

  • Families clearing a parent's or relative's home after a passing
  • Probate attorneys who need a property cleared quickly for estate settlement
  • Real estate agents preparing a property for listing or sale
  • Property managers clearing units after long-term tenants
  • Executors and trustees managing estate assets and property disposition
  • Senior living coordinators helping residents transition to assisted living

What We Remove During an Estate Cleanout

We clear everything that isn't staying with the family or being sold. A typical Miami estate cleanout includes:

  • All furniture — beds, dressers, sofas, dining sets, wardrobes
  • Appliances — refrigerators, washers, dryers, stoves, microwaves
  • Clothing, linens, and personal effects (bagged and donated when possible)
  • Kitchenware, dishes, and pantry contents
  • Books, décor, and miscellaneous household items
  • Garage contents — tools, equipment, stored items
  • Yard and outdoor furniture
  • Trash, debris, and leftover renovation materials

We sort as we go — donating usable items to Miami-area charities and disposing of everything else responsibly.

How the Estate Cleanout Process Works

We've streamlined estate cleanouts into a clear, five-step process so families and professionals always know what to expect:

  1. Text us photos of the property. Send pictures of each room, the garage, and any outdoor areas to (305) 928-5773. Include the address, approximate square footage, and any deadline you're working against (probate date, closing date, etc.).
  2. We send a firm quote. We review the photos, estimate volume and labor, and text you back a price range — usually within 10 minutes. For larger estates, we may schedule a brief walkthrough before finalizing.
  3. Our crew arrives on your scheduled day. We confirm a 1–2 hour arrival window. You don't need to be present the entire time if you've pre-approved scope, but someone should be available at the start and end of the job.
  4. We clear the entire property. Our team removes everything agreed upon — room by room, including garage and yard. We donate usable items, bag personal effects for your review when requested, and haul away the rest.
  5. Final walkthrough and sign-off. When the job is done, we walk through the property with you (or your agent/attorney) to confirm every room is clear. You pay the agreed price — no surprise fees.

Frequently Asked Questions — Estate Cleanout Miami

How long does an estate cleanout take?

Most studio and 1–2 bedroom estates are cleared in 3–6 hours. A full 3–4 bedroom home with garage typically takes one full day. Larger estates with heavy accumulation or hoarding conditions may require two days — we'll give you an accurate estimate after reviewing photos or doing a walkthrough.

Do I need to sort anything before you arrive?

No. You can sort items you want to keep, sell, or donate before we arrive if you'd like — but it's not required. Many families mark rooms or items to keep, and we work around those. If you haven't had time to sort, we can bag personal effects and set aside anything that looks valuable or sentimental for your review.

Can you work with a probate attorney or real estate agent?

Yes — we work with probate attorneys, estate executors, and real estate agents regularly. We can coordinate directly with your attorney or agent on scheduling, scope, and final walkthrough. We also provide documentation of completion if needed for court or closing records.

What happens to items still in good condition?

Usable furniture, clothing, kitchenware, and household goods are donated to local Miami charities and thrift organizations whenever possible. We sort as we load — donations go one direction, disposal another. If your family wants specific items donated to a particular organization, let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Do you handle hoarding situations?

Yes. Some estate cleanouts involve significant accumulation that goes beyond a typical cleanout. We handle these situations with extra discretion and no judgment. For properties with severe hoarding conditions, see our dedicated hoarding cleanout service — we bring additional crew and time to clear the property thoroughly.

Can you clean the property after the cleanout?

Yes. Once everything is removed, we can coordinate a move-out cleaning so the property is ready for listing, showing, or turnover. Many real estate agents book the cleanout and cleaning together so the home is market-ready in a single visit.
