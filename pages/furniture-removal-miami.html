How We Protect Your Property During Furniture Removal

Furniture removal is not just about getting items out the door — it is about getting them out without leaving damage behind. Miami properties present unique challenges: high-rise condos with freight elevators and strict move-out windows, older homes in Coral Gables with narrow doorways and original hardwood floors, townhouses in Kendall with tight stairwells, and HOA-managed communities that require proof of licensed hauling before they will open the loading dock.

Our crew uses door jamb protectors on every job to prevent scratches and dents as furniture passes through doorways. We lay floor coverings in hallways and entryways when dragging is unavoidable. For heavy items on upper floors, we carry — we do not drag — and we use furniture sliders and straps to control movement through tight turns.

In Miami Beach and Brickell high-rises, we coordinate with building management on freight elevator timing, loading dock access, and certificate of insurance requirements when needed. We know the drill: reserve the elevator, protect the common areas, and be off the property before the next resident's move-in window. We have done this hundreds of times across Miami-Dade and Broward.

If your furniture will not fit in the elevator or through a doorway without disassembly, we handle that on-site. Bed frames come apart. Sectionals break into modules. Table legs come off. We reassemble nothing — we just get it out safely and leave your walls, floors, and door frames exactly as we found them.

Frequently Asked Questions — Furniture Removal Miami

Do I need to disassemble furniture before you arrive?

No. Our crew handles disassembly on-site when needed — bed frames, sectionals, table legs, and other modular furniture. You do not need to prepare anything beyond clearing a path to the item and letting us know about any access challenges (stairs, elevator size, narrow doorways) when you book. If you have already disassembled something, that is fine too — it may reduce labor time slightly but it is never required.

Can you remove furniture from a high-rise condo in Miami Beach?

Yes. We regularly remove furniture from high-rise condos across Miami Beach, Brickell, Sunny Isles, and Aventura. We coordinate freight elevator reservations with building management when required, bring certificate of insurance when HOAs request it, and work within your building's move-out windows. Let us know your building name and floor when you text for a quote so we can plan accordingly.

What if my furniture won't fit in the elevator?

We encounter this regularly in Miami condos. Our crew will assess on arrival and determine the best approach — disassembling the item, carrying it via stairs, or using the freight elevator if it has larger dimensions than the passenger elevator. For extremely large items like sectionals or king bed frames, we often disassemble on-site and carry components separately. We will never force an item into an elevator and risk damage to the furniture or the building.

Do you donate furniture that's still in good condition?

Yes. Furniture in usable condition — couches without major damage, functional dressers, clean mattresses, working office chairs — is donated to Miami-area charities and thrift organizations whenever possible. Let us know when you book if you would like us to prioritize donation for specific items. We cannot guarantee donation acceptance for every piece (charities have their own condition standards) but we always try before hauling to disposal.

Can you remove just one piece of furniture?

Absolutely. Single-item pickups are one of our most common requests — one couch, one mattress, one dresser, one recliner. Single large items start at $99. Text us a photo of the item and your address and we will confirm exact pricing and same-day availability within minutes.

How do I get a price without you coming to my home first?

Text us photos. Send pictures of the furniture you need removed, including shots that show scale (next to a door or wall helps) and any access challenges like stairs or tight hallways. We price most standard furniture removal jobs from photos alone and text you a firm quote — usually within 10 minutes. For unusual items like pianos, safes, or extremely large custom pieces, we may ask a follow-up question or schedule a quick walkthrough before finalizing.
