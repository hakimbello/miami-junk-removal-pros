The Difference Between Short Term and Mid Term Rental Cleaning

Not all short-term rental stays are the same — and the cleaning protocol should match the stay type.

Short-term stays (1–29 nights) require fast turnovers with a full reset every single time a guest checks out. Guest checks out at 11AM, next guest arrives at 3PM — four hours to transform the unit from lived-in to hotel-ready. Every surface, every linen, every supply level must be reset. This is our core service and our highest-volume work across Miami.

Mid-term stays (30+ nights) are common on Furnished Finder, corporate housing platforms, and extended Airbnb bookings. Guests live in the property for a month or more — cooking, showering, using every room daily. Check-out requires a more thorough deep clean than a standard turnover: inside appliances, grout scrubbing, closet clearing, and attention to areas that accumulate wear over weeks of daily use. We handle mid-term check-out deep cleans with a separate protocol designed for the additional scope.

We handle both with appropriate protocols for each stay type. Tell us your booking type when you schedule and we will apply the right checklist and pricing for your turnover.

Miami STR Regulations and What They Mean for Your Cleaning

Short-term rental regulation in Miami has tightened significantly in recent years. Miami-Dade County and the City of Miami both require short-term rental operators to obtain licenses, register properties, and maintain units to standards that can be inspected by county or city officials.

Licensed STR hosts in Miami-Dade must maintain their properties in safe, sanitary condition. Inspection failures — including cleanliness deficiencies, pest issues, or maintenance problems identified during a county inspection — can result in fines, license suspension, or revocation. For hosts operating legally under a Miami-Dade or City of Miami STR license, professional cleaning documentation supports compliance by demonstrating that the property is maintained to required standards between guest stays.

We provide completion photos after every cleaning that serve as dated records of property condition. For hosts who need documentation for license renewals, insurance claims, or dispute resolution with guests or platforms, our photo documentation provides a timestamped record of the unit's condition at turnover.

We do not provide legal advice on STR licensing — but we understand that professional cleaning is a operational requirement for licensed hosts, not an optional expense. Maintaining your license means maintaining your property to a standard that passes inspection, and that starts with every turnover.

Short-term rental cleaning operates on turnover timelines measured in hours, not days. Every checkout requires a full reset to hotel standard — fresh linens, restocked supplies, inside appliances, grout scrubbing, and photo documentation — not just tidying a home someone continues to live in. Regular house cleaners are not trained for four-hour turnaround windows, platform review standards, or the documentation hosts need after every turnover. STR cleaning is a specialized discipline with different tools, checklists, and urgency than residential maintenance cleaning.
