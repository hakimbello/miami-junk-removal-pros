Move Out Cleaning vs. Regular Cleaning — What's Different

Regular house cleaning maintains a home that people are actively living in. The cleaner works around your belongings, focuses on visible surfaces, and keeps things tidy week to week. It is maintenance — keeping a lived-in space presentable.

Move out cleaning is a deep clean with a different standard entirely. The unit must be cleaned as if no one has ever lived there. That means inside the oven, inside the refrigerator, inside every cabinet, behind the toilet, in the shower grout, on top of ceiling fans, and along every baseboard. Landlords and property managers in Miami inspect every one of these areas — often with a checklist and a flashlight.

The scope difference is significant. A regular weekly cleaning for a two-bedroom apartment might take 90 minutes and cover surfaces at eye level. A move out cleaning for the same unit takes three to five hours and covers every surface from floor to ceiling, inside every appliance, and through every closet. The detail level is what separates a deposit returned in full from a $200 cleaning deduction on your move-out report.

Time, products, and technique all differ. Move out cleaning requires degreasers for kitchen buildup, grout scrubbers for bathrooms, appliance-safe cleaners for oven and fridge interiors, and the patience to work through a 40-point checklist without cutting corners. That is what Miami Junk Removal Pros delivers on every move out job.

For Tenants — How to Maximize Your Security Deposit Return

Getting your full security deposit back in Miami requires more than a good cleaning — it requires documentation, timing, and knowing what your landlord will inspect. Here is what we recommend to every tenant we work with:

Document the condition before cleaning. Take photos and video of every room before our crew arrives — and after your belongings are removed. This creates a record of what you are responsible for versus what was pre-existing wear and tear. Florida law distinguishes between damage and normal wear; documentation protects you if a dispute arises.

Remove all personal items first. Our move out cleaning covers the unit, not your belongings. Everything you own must be out before we start — furniture, boxes, closet contents, refrigerator food, bathroom items. A unit full of leftover items cannot be deep cleaned to inspection standard.

Book professional cleaning at least two days before your final walkthrough. Do not schedule cleaning the morning of your move-out inspection. Give the unit time to air out, let floors dry completely, and allow yourself a buffer if any area needs a touch-up. Landlords notice wet floors and chemical smells from cleaning done 30 minutes before they arrive.

Check your lease for specific cleaning requirements. Some Miami leases specify professional carpet cleaning, oven cleaning, or other requirements beyond standard move-out scope. Read your lease before booking so we can include those items in your quote.

Ask your landlord for a move-out checklist in advance. Many property managers provide a written checklist of exactly what they inspect. Share it with us when you book and we will make sure every item on their list is covered.

Know your rights under Florida law. Florida Statute § 83.49 requires landlords to return security deposits within 15 to 60 days of move-out, depending on whether deductions are claimed. Landlords must provide an itemized list of any deductions. Normal wear and tear — faded paint, minor carpet wear, small nail holes — cannot be deducted. Cleaning deductions are only valid when the unit is left unreasonably dirty beyond normal use. A professional move out cleaning eliminates the most common legitimate deduction landlords claim.
