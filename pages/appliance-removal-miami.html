Safe Disconnection and Responsible Disposal

Appliance removal is not just about getting the unit out your door. What happens after we haul it away matters — for the environment, for Miami-Dade County compliance, and for your peace of mind.

Refrigerators, freezers, and air conditioning units contain refrigerants (commonly known as freon) that are regulated under the Clean Air Act. These chemicals cannot be released into the atmosphere or dumped in a landfill. Before any appliance containing refrigerant is disposed of, the refrigerant must be recovered by a certified technician using EPA-approved equipment. Miami Junk Removal Pros works with certified recovery providers in South Florida to ensure every unit is handled legally.

Beyond refrigerants, appliances contain valuable recyclable materials — steel, copper, aluminum, and other metals that should be recovered rather than buried. We route appliances to certified recyclers in the Miami area who strip metal components for recycling and dispose of non-recyclable materials according to Miami-Dade County solid waste regulations.

We do not cut corners on disposal. Illegal dumping of appliances — especially refrigerators — is a serious environmental violation in Florida, and property owners can be held liable if their contractor dumps irresponsibly. When you hire Miami Junk Removal Pros, you get documentation that your appliances were disposed of through proper channels. That matters for landlords, property managers, and anyone clearing a unit for sale or re-rental.

Frequently Asked Questions — Appliance Removal Miami

Do you disconnect the appliances or do I need to do that first?

We handle disconnection as part of the job. Our crew disconnects water supply lines on washers and dishwashers, caps gas lines on gas stoves and water heaters, and unplugs electrical connections before moving any appliance. You do not need to hire a plumber or handyman before we arrive. If an appliance requires work beyond standard disconnection — such as hardwired electrical or complex gas line modifications — we will let you know during the quoting process so there are no surprises on arrival.

How do you dispose of refrigerators and appliances with freon?

Refrigerators, freezers, and air conditioning units contain regulated refrigerants that must be recovered before disposal. We route these appliances to certified recovery facilities in South Florida where EPA-certified technicians extract the refrigerant using approved equipment. The unit is then processed for metal recycling. This process is required by federal law and Miami-Dade County waste regulations — we never dump refrigerant-containing appliances in landfills.

Can you remove a gas stove safely?

Yes. Our crew shuts off the gas supply at the appliance connection, disconnects the gas line, and caps the fitting before moving the stove. We handle both natural gas and propane connections. If you smell gas when we arrive or suspect a leak, do not wait for our appointment — call your gas utility emergency line first, then contact us once the situation is safe. For standard gas stove removal, we take care of the full disconnection and haul-away in one visit.
